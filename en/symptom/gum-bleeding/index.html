When to see a doctor

Seek care promptly if "Bleeding Gums" suddenly worsens, lasts more than two weeks, comes with fever/chest pain/breathing trouble/confusion, or disrupts daily life.

FAQ

Can supplements fix Bleeding Gums?+
Rarely by themselves. At best they are supportive nutrition topics; root causes need lifestyle change and sometimes medical evaluation.
Why is Vitamin C mentioned?+
Vitamin C often appears in public health info and searches related to Bleeding Gums. Suitability depends on your situation and clinician advice.
How long until I notice anything?+
It varies. Some notice subjective changes in days; many nutrients need weeks. Worsening means stop and seek care.
